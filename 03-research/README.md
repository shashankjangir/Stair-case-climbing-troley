# Research

## Current research — 10 October 2026

Read the [ten-design literature review and CAD design basis](trolley-research-and-cad-design-basis.md). It contains the source links, published calculations and corrections, Indian stair references, our preliminary calculations, material choices, modelling dimensions and test targets.

The selected CAD starting point is a manual paired-plate tri-star trolley with a 50 kg payload target, 200 mm rubber wheels and 190 mm carrier radius. The review distinguishes selected dimensions from validated performance. The earlier research questions and original-build lessons below remain useful context.

The research should help me turn the tri-star concept into a design that fits the stairs, carries the intended load, and can be built with the tools available.

## Questions to answer

| Topic | What I need to find out | Where the answer will be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tri-star geometry | How wheel radius, carrier radius, and wheel spacing affect the transition between steps. | Mechanism sketches and CAD. |
| Stair profiles | Which riser heights, tread depths, edge shapes, and widths the design must handle. | Requirements and clearance checks. |
| Mechanism layout | How the carrier and individual wheels rotate, and how they connect to the chassis. | Shaft, hub, and bearing design. |
| Torque and pull force | Which positions require the most effort and which assumptions a calculation needs. | Mechanical calculations and test planning. |
| Load distribution and centre of gravity | How payload position and trolley tilt affect balance during successive steps. | Platform position, handle layout, and stability checks. |
| Structural design | How to select frame sections, shafts, joints, wheels, and bearings for the chosen loads. | Component selection and fabrication. |
| Testing | How to measure pull force, deflection, load movement, and stair transitions consistently. | Prototype evaluation. |

## Lessons from the earlier build

The [MCP101 report](../07-documentation/original-prototype/report/mcp101-project-report.docx) is now a project reference. It identifies the tri-wheel layout and describes the fabrication approach, stair trials, and practical issues found during the work.

Research for the rebuild should give particular attention to heavier-load tilting, wheel grip on different surfaces, wheel spacing, handle position, and material cost. The report mentions aluminium or mild steel as frame options and rubberized wheels, but does not specify the final material grades or component specifications. Those choices still need to be established for the rebuild.

Its general claims about smooth motion and lower effort provide questions to investigate; numerical comparisons will come from the current calculations and tests.
