## Information Shashank can provide next

1. The incident or observation that originally inspired the project.
2. Test staircase measurements: each rise and going, clear width, nosing and landing dimensions; dry/wet surface details.
3. Original prototype dimensions, especially axle-to-frame offset, wheel sizes, carrier radius, frame sections and handle position. Label estimates separately from measurements.
4. Approximate budget, available workshop tools and access to welding/machining.
5. Original group members, individual contributions and any additional genuine build/trial photographs or videos.

These are information gaps, not requests to rush fabrication or perform loaded tests before the design checks.
